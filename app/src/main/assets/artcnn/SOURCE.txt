ArtCNN_C4F16_DS.glsl and ArtCNN_C4F32_DS.glsl are generated from ONNX/ArtCNN_C4F16_DS.onnx and
ONNX/ArtCNN_C4F32_DS.onnx of commit 8789539dfae120f2877a89bb6218026d56809612 (release v1.6.x, DS weights of 2025-09)
with scripts/artcnn_glsl.py, as fragment shaders: upstream ships these models only as compute
shaders, which libplacebo's OpenGL ES backend (Android) cannot run. The layout is the one of the
last upstream fragment-shader release (d2f645a). ArtCNN_C4F32_DS.glsl computes in mediump (fp16
on mobile GPUs, as upstream's compute shaders use float16 when available); its output differs
from the ONNX model by less than 1/255 away from frame borders.
